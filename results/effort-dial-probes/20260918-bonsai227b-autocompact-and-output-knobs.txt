WHY IT MATTERS ARITHMETICALLY
  llama-server budgets prompt + completion against n_ctx. Raising the compaction trigger to
  54,067 (60% of 90,112) puts a worst-case overshoot turn near 79K of prompt; with the CLI's
  default 32,000 ask that is ~111K against a 98,304 server, i.e. a hard error hours into a leg.
  With 8,192 it is ~87K, inside the ceiling. Largest single-turn thinking burst measured on this
  arm: 3,257 tokens, so 8,192 is not a constraint on the model.

OPEN, AND NOT THIS ARM'S TO SETTLE
  Every Claude row on this board inherited PCT=30 silently. On a 200K or 1M window that is 60K or
  300K and nobody noticed; on any future small-window arm it is the difference between a score and
  a DNF. Worth stating per-arm rather than inheriting from a file no receipt records.
